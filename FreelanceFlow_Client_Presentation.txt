## 🚀 Overview

FreelanceFlow is a powerful, all-in-one platform designed specifically for freelancers to manage their entire business workflow. From client management to invoicing, project tracking to expense monitoring - we've got you covered.

**Built with:** React, Node.js, MongoDB, Vercel, Render

---

## 💼 Key Features

### 1. Client Management
- Add and manage client profiles with complete contact information
- Track client communication history
- View all projects and invoices per client
- Quick client lookup and search

### 2. Project Management
- Create projects with detailed descriptions
- Set project status (Planning, Active, On Hold, Completed)
- Assign budgets and deadlines
- Track time spent on each project
- Link projects to specific clients

### 3. Invoice Generation
- Create professional PDF invoices
- Customizable invoice templates
- Multiple currency support (INR, USD, EUR)
- GSTIN integration for Indian freelancers
- Track invoice status (Draft, Sent, Paid, Overdue)
- Payment link generation

### 4. Proposal & Contract Management
- Create professional proposals
- Contract templates
- Track proposal status
- E-signature ready

### 5. Lead Tracking
- Manage potential clients
- Pipeline view for sales process
- Lead scoring and prioritization
- Follow-up reminders

### 6. Task Management
- Create and assign tasks
- Set priorities (Low, Medium, High, Urgent)
- Due date tracking
- Status updates (Todo, In Progress, Review, Done)

### 7. Time Tracking
- Log billable hours
- Multiple project support
- Weekly/monthly time reports
- Export time logs

### 8. Expense Management
- Track business expenses
- Categorize expenses
- Receipt documentation
- Monthly/yearly expense reports

### 9. Payment Tracking
- Record all payments received
- Link payments to invoices
- Payment history
- Revenue reports

### 10. AI Assistant
- Smart suggestions for client communication
- Invoice optimization tips
- Business growth recommendations
- Automated responses

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The main dashboard provides:
- **Total Revenue** - Sum of all payments received
- **Pending Invoices** - Outstanding payments to collect
- **Active Projects** - Currently running projects
- **Recent Leads** - Latest potential clients

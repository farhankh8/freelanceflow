## SLIDE 13: MATHEMATICAL LOGIC

# Invoice Calculations

```
Subtotal = Σ (Quantity × Rate)
Tax Amount = Subtotal × (Tax Rate / 100)
Total = Subtotal + Tax Amount
Earnings = Duration × Hourly Rate
```

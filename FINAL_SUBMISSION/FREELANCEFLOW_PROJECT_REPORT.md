CHAPTER 1: INTRODUCTION

1.1 Background

The freelance economy has witnessed exponential growth. Freelancers face challenges in managing their business efficiently including client management, invoicing, time tracking, and financial planning. FreelanceFlow emerges as a comprehensive solution to address these challenges.

1.2 Objectives

1. Centralized Client Management
2. Project Tracking & Management
3. Automated Invoice Generation
4. Time Tracking & Billing
5. Financial Reporting
6. Lead & Proposal Management

1.3 Purpose

To develop a robust web application that helps freelancers save time, increase revenue, maintain professional image, gain insights, and stay organized.

1.4 Scope

Present: Individual accounts, CRUD operations, PDF invoices, Dashboard, Lead tracking, Responsive design

Excluded: Team features, Mobile apps, Payment gateway, Notifications

1.5 Data Sources

User data, Client data, Project data, Invoice data, Time log data, Expense data, Lead data, Proposal data

1.6 Problem Definition

Problem: Fragmented business management leading to wasted time, missed payments, undercharging, scattered info

Solution: All-in-one platform consolidating operations
